One-shot Interface, operation-graph authority, purpose-owned projections

    
Combine the strongest parts instead of selecting a proposal unchanged. Keep one private derive operation and one opaque VerifiedCoreProgram. Inside the Module, make the closed typed operation graph plus its centralized operation-law catalog authoritative. Keep proof and rewrite evidence explicit enough for independent verification, but keep pass orchestration and intermediate revisions private. Derive the function/region catalog, Flow input, ownership and capacity requirements, backend input, inspection, and receipt as purpose-owned projections. ImagePlan remains the final whole-Image admission authority.
