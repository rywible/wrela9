One-shot interface, obligation-first authority, resume-catalog projections

    
Combine the strongest parts rather than picking a proposal unchanged. Keep one private derive operation and one opaque verified result. Make the semantic event-and-obligation graph authoritative inside the Module because it gives the verifier and scheduler model the strongest independent footing. Generate the resume catalog, Core resume functions, demand ledger, inspection, and model contract as purpose-owned projections. Keep final capacity, placement, service-plan, and Logical Image Layout admission in ImagePlan.
